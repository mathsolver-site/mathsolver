# MathSolver

Free math solver with 49 step-by-step calculators — algebra, calculus, fractions, geometry, statistics. Solve equations, derivatives, integrals and more with steps shown.

Visit: https://mathsolver.site/

## What it does

MathSolver helps students and learners solve math problems with clear, step-by-step explanations. Instead of only showing an answer, it shows how to reach it, so you can learn the method and check your work.

## Calculators

MathSolver includes 49 calculators covering:

- Algebra — equations, systems, polynomials, and expressions
- Calculus — derivatives, integrals, and limits
- Fractions — simplification, operations, and conversions
- Geometry — area, perimeter, volume, and angles
- Statistics — averages, probability, and data summaries

## Why use MathSolver

- Free to use
- Step-by-step solutions
- Covers school, college, and everyday math
- Works in the browser, no install needed
